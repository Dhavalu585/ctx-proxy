Your data

Your keys and content stay yours.

CtxCrush handles sensitive traffic — your provider keys, prompts, and documents. Here's exactly what happens to them.

Provider keys are never stored

Your API key passes through to your provider and identifies your account only as a one-way hash. The raw key is never written to disk or logs.

Never used for marketing or training

Prompts and uploaded documents are processed only to compress and recall them for you. They are not sold, shared, used to train models, or used for any marketing.

Indexed for you, isolated to you

Uploaded documents and conversation memory are scoped to your account and searchable only by you. One account's content is never visible to another.

Encrypted in transit

All traffic to CtxCrush runs over HTTPS. Your keys and content are encrypted on the wire between your tools and the proxy.
